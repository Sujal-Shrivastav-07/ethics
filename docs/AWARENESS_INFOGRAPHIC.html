✓ Essential Cyber Safety Do's
  • Verify Financial Advisors: Check advisor registration exclusively on the official sebi.gov.in portal.
  • Family Safe Word: Establish a confidential verbal offline password to verify AI voice and video clone calls.
  • App-Based 2FA: Use Google or Microsoft Authenticator apps instead of SMS OTPs to block SIM swap attacks.
  • Masked Aadhaar: Use Masked Aadhaar (displaying only the last 4 digits) for all non-statutory KYC submissions.
✗ Critical Cyber Safety Don'ts
  • Never Share OTP or UPI PIN: Entering your UPI PIN always DEBITS money from your account, never credits it.
  • Never Grant Blanket Permissions: Deny contacts, storage, and gallery permissions to instant loan utilities.
  • Never Transfer to "Verification Accounts": No government body maintains safe verification bank accounts.
  • Never Trust Sideloaded Apps: Avoid downloading enterprise iOS profiles or third-party APKs outside official app stores.
