* **Why we made it:** To give faculty and citizens an immediate macro-level overview of organized cyber syndicates in India before drilling down into individual cases.

### 📚 Page 2: I4C Case Library
* **What it is:** A searchable, category-filtered database of the 8 verified cases. Clicking **"View Full Dossier"** opens a modal with all 14 analysis parameters.
* **Why we made it:** To fulfill the core syllabus research mandate. It proves you did not just write a generic summary, but conducted structured forensic modeling.

### ⚠️ Page 3: "Is This a Scam?" Diagnostic Analyzer
* **What it is:** An interactive decision-support tool where users check observable red flags (e.g., threats of arrest, AnyDesk installation, promises of 500% returns). It calculates an instant threat score (0 to 100) and displays immediate defensive protocols.
* **Why we made it:** This is the **core innovation**. Panicked citizens do not read 50-page legal reports when being scammed; they need a rapid 30-second diagnostic tool to tell them to hang up and dial 1930.

### 🛡️ Page 4: Cyber Hygiene Self-Audit Tool
* **What it is:** An interactive 10-point checklist covering Aadhaar biometric locking, app authenticators, APK avoidance, and family safe words. It calculates a dynamic percentage score.
* **Why we made it:** To empower citizens with proactive self-defense rather than reactive panic.

### ⚖️ Page 5: Indian Cyber Law Matrix
* **What it is:** A legal explorer breaking down **IT Act 2000 (Sec 43, 66, 66C, 66D, 66E)** and the newly enacted **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita 2023 (Sec 318, 319, 308, 336, 204)** with simple "Student-Friendly Explanations".

### 🧠 Page 6: Digital Ethics & Governance Hub
* **What it is:** A framework detailing the 4 Ethical Pillars (Privacy by Design, Consent Integrity, Protecting Vulnerable Users, and Algorithmic Anti-Deception) alongside a Developer's Code of Ethics.

### 🚨 Page 7: Emergency Incident Response (1930)
* **What it is:** A step-by-step roadmap explaining what to do after being defrauded, emphasizing the **2-Hour Golden Window** and how calling **1930** activates the Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting System (CFCFRS) to freeze bank funds.
* **Why we made it:** To bridge academic research with actionable public safety.

### 🎨 Page 8: Citizen Awareness Infographics

## 5. TOP 5 TOUGH QUESTIONS MA'AM MIGHT ASK & HOW TO ANSWER

### Q1: *"What is the newly enacted law for cyber fraud in India?"*
* **Answer:** *"Ma'am, it is the **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS)**, which replaced the Indian Penal Code (IPC). For instance, the classic IPC Section 420 for cheating is now **Section 318(4) of BNS**, carrying up to 7 years imprisonment. Additionally, impersonating police officers is booked under **BNS Section 204**, and extortion is booked under **BNS Section 308**."*

### Q2: *"Why can't police simply trace Digital Arrest calls?"*
* **Answer:** *"Because syndicates utilize international VoIP caller ID spoofing and route traffic through encrypted messaging services. The calls originate from overseas boiler rooms (often across Southeast Asia), and stolen funds are layered within minutes across dozens of domestic mule accounts before being converted to cryptocurrency."*

### Q3: *"What is the 'Golden Hour' in cybercrime?"*
* **Answer:** *"The Golden Hour refers to the first **120 minutes (2 hours)** following unauthorized financial debit. If the victim calls National Helpline **1930**, the CFCFRS portal triggers an automated inter-bank freeze signal across participating Indian banks, blocking the stolen money before criminals can withdraw cash at ATMs."*

### Q4: *"How does Aadhaar biometric locking prevent AePS fraud?"*
* **Answer:** *"In AePS fraud, scammers use synthetic silicone rubber thumbs created from public land records. When a citizen locks their biometrics in the mAadhaar app or UIDAI portal, the UIDAI authentication server immediately rejects any fingerprint match request until the citizen explicitly unlocks it. This eliminates silicone fingerprint theft even if the scammer has the citizen's Aadhaar number and thumbprint."*

### Q5: *"Why did you choose a web app instead of an Android APK?"*
* **Answer:** *"Two reasons, Ma'am: First, accessibility and demonstration—a responsive web application runs instantly on any laptop, projector, or mobile device without requiring emulator configuration or downloading untrusted APKs. Second, our case research revealed that malicious APK sideloading is one of the top cyber threat vectors in India (Case 4 and Case 7). Therefore, building a secure, zero-permission web app is itself an example of responsible, ethical software design."*
