## 2. ANTICIPATED FACULTY VIVA QUESTIONS & MODEL ANSWERS

### Q1: "What is the difference between IT Act Section 66C and Section 66D?"
* **Your Answer:**  
  *"Ma'am, Section 66C of the IT Act, 2000 specifically penalizes **Identity Theft**—which is the fraudulent or dishonest use of someone else's electronic signature, password, biometric trait, or unique identification feature.  
  In contrast, Section 66D penalizes **Cheating by Personation using a computer resource**—which applies when an attacker pretends to be someone else (such as a CBI officer, bank executive, or customs official) to deceive the victim and obtain money or valuable property."*

### Q2: "What is 'Digital Arrest' under Indian Law?"
* **Your Answer:**  
  *"Ma'am, 'Digital Arrest' is a **complete legal fiction created by cyber fraudsters**. Indian procedural law—neither the Code of Criminal Procedure (CrPC) nor the newly enacted Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS)—contains any provision for placing a citizen under arrest or conducting an interrogation via Skype, WhatsApp, or any video call. Legitimate law enforcement agencies such as CBI, ED, or State Police never demand money transfers for verification or asset clearance."*

### Q3: "How does the AePS biometric spoofing attack work without an OTP?"
* **Your Answer:**  
  *"The Aadhaar Enabled Payment System (AePS) was designed for financial inclusion in rural areas where citizens may not have smartphones or remember passwords. It requires only an Aadhaar number and a biometric fingerprint match on an optical Point of Sale scanner.  
  Fraudsters exploit two flaws: first, public land registry deeds uploaded online contain visible, unredacted thumbprints. Second, low-cost optical scanners lack **hardware liveness detection** (which checks for blood circulation or body temperature). Fraudsters cast synthetic silicone rubber thumbs and debit accounts up to ₹10,000 per transaction without needing an OTP."*

### Q4: "What changes did the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS) bring to cyber fraud prosecution?"
* **Your Answer:**  
  *"The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 replaced the Indian Penal Code, 1860. The key changes relevant to cybercrimes include:
  1. **IPC Section 420** (Cheating) is now **BNS Section 318(4)** with rigorous imprisonment up to 7 years.
  2. **IPC Section 170** (Impersonating a public servant) is now **BNS Section 204**.
  3. **IPC Section 383/384** (Extortion) is now **BNS Section 308**.
  4. **IPC Section 468** (Forgery for cheating) is now **BNS Section 336**."*

### Q5: "What is Privacy by Design in your project?"
* **Your Answer:**  
  *"In CyberPrahari, Privacy by Design means that security and privacy are built into the architecture from the first line of code. Our web application does not collect user names, phone numbers, or passwords. All scam diagnostic calculations execute 100% locally in the browser with zero external server tracking, zero cookies, and zero third-party telemetry."*
